## Important
Score persona-specific distinguishability, not superficial difference. Different wording, length, politeness, or formatting is not enough for a high score unless the difference is tied to persona contract behavior such as knowledge boundary, question style, emotional profile, or behavioral rules.

## Score Ceiling Rules
- If the two sets are indistinguishable, distinctiveness must be 1.
- If differences are only superficial, distinctiveness cannot exceed 2.
- If both sets are generic students, distinctiveness cannot exceed 3 even if wording differs.
- Scores 4-5 require contract-relevant persona evidence.

## Scoring Criteria (1-5 scale)
1 = Indistinguishable. ANY of the following: (a) no meaningful behavioral difference; (b) both sets are generic; (c) differences are too small to support persona-conditioned value; (d) a human reviewer could not tell which set is persona-conditioned.

2 = Weak distinction. No Score 1 failure, but at least 2 of the following: (a) differences are mostly wording or length; (b) persona-specific evidence is weak; (c) both sets ask similar generic questions; (d) emotional tone is similar; (e) knowledge boundary is not clearly different.

3 = Moderate distinction. ALL of the following: (a) some behavioral difference is visible; (b) at least one difference is plausibly persona-related; (c) no Score 1 failure; (d) generic overlap remains substantial.

4 = Strong distinction. Meets all Score 3 baselines, plus at least 2 of the following: (a) persona-conditioned set shows clearer knowledge-boundary behavior; (b) question style differs in a contract-relevant way; (c) emotional profile differs meaningfully; (d) generic overlap is minor; (e) persona_value_add can cite specific evidence.

5 = Excellent distinction. Meets all Score 4 criteria, plus at least 2 of the following: (a) persona-conditioned behavior is consistently distinguishable; (b) differences span knowledge boundary, question style, and emotional/behavioral profile; (c) generic set lacks those persona-specific behaviors; (d) distinction is obvious to a human reviewer; (e) no major generic behavior appears in the persona-conditioned set.

## Evaluation Axes
- distinctiveness: How different are the two sets in persona-specific behavior? (1-5)
- persona_value_add: What specific contract-relevant behaviors differentiate the persona-conditioned set from the generic set?

## Failure Taxonomy
If persona-conditioned behavior is weak, classify it using zero or more of:
generic_student_behavior, emotional_mismatch, under_competence, persona_contract_contradiction.

Use failure types consistently:
- generic_student_behavior prevents scores 4-5.
- emotional_mismatch lowers distinctiveness if persona tone is not visible.
- under_competence lowers distinctiveness if it contradicts the intended persona.
- persona_contract_contradiction usually limits distinctiveness to <= 2.
