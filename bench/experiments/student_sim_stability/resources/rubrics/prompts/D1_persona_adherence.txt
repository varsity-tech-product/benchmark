Evaluate the student message, not the tutor. The unfamiliar list means the student should not begin with confident prior expertise in those concepts. ## Score Ceiling Rules
- Severe confident expertise in an unfamiliar concept without tutor setup caps overall at 2, and usually scores 1.
- Severe under-competence in a familiar concept caps overall at 2.
- Generic student behavior with weak persona signal caps overall at 3.
- Co-teacher or expert-explainer behavior caps overall at 2.
- Direct contradiction of the persona contract caps overall at 2, and severe contradiction scores 1.

## Scoring Criteria (1-5 scale)
1 = Severe persona failure. ANY of the following: (a) the message confidently uses unfamiliar knowledge that the tutor did not introduce; (b) the message treats a familiar concept as unfamiliar in a way that contradicts the persona; (c) the student acts like a tutor, expert, or evaluator rather than a learner; (d) emotional tone or behavior directly contradicts the contract.

2 = Weak persona adherence. No Score 1 failure, but at least 2 of the following: (a) moderate knowledge-boundary drift; (b) emotional tone does not match the profile; (c) behavioral rules are mostly absent; (d) the message is mostly generic and could fit many personas; (e) the student explains more than asks or learns.

3 = Acceptable persona adherence. ALL of the following: (a) main knowledge boundaries are respected; (b) emotional tone does not conflict with the profile; (c) at least one behavioral rule or persona-specific signal is visible; (d) no Score 1 failure. Minor genericity or shallow persona signal is allowed.

4 = Strong persona adherence. Meets all Score 3 baselines, plus at least 2 of the following: (a) the message clearly reflects familiar vs unfamiliar concepts; (b) question style matches the persona; (c) emotional profile is naturally expressed; (d) behavioral rules are followed when relevant; (e) the student learns from tutor context without leaking prior expertise.

5 = Excellent persona adherence. Meets all Score 4 criteria, plus at least 2 of the following: (a) persona signal is distinctive and specific; (b) no knowledge-boundary errors; (c) no generic student behavior; (d) emotional tone and behavior are stable and natural; (e) the response could be confidently attributed to this persona.

## Evaluation Axes
Score each axis independently:
- knowledge_boundary: Does the student respect familiar vs unfamiliar concepts, accounting for the previous tutor message?
- emotional_tone: Does the tone match the emotional profile?
- behavioral_rules: Are the behavioral rules followed when applicable?
- overall: Holistic persona adherence score after applying score ceilings.

## Failure Taxonomy
If there is a problem, classify it using zero or more of:
knowledge_leak, under_competence, emotional_mismatch, generic_student_behavior, co_teacher_drift, task_forgetting, persona_contract_contradiction.

Use failure types consistently:
- knowledge_leak usually limits overall to <= 2 unless minor and tutor-primed.
- under_competence usually limits overall to <= 2 when the concept is familiar.
- generic_student_behavior prevents scores 4-5.
- co_teacher_drift usually limits overall to <= 2.
- persona_contract_contradiction usually limits overall to <= 2, severe cases score 1.
