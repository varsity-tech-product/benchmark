## Important
Only evaluate lines labeled "Scored student turn". Lines labeled "Context only" are included so you can tell whether the tutor introduced a concept before the student referred to it.

The unfamiliar list means the student should not begin with confident prior expertise in those concepts. It does not forbid normal learning during the session. Student messages may ask about, echo, or paraphrase concepts that the tutor introduced in preceding context turns. For a given scored student turn, only earlier context can justify that turn; later tutor turns must not retroactively excuse it.

## Mixed Direction Warning
- persona_fidelity is high-good: 1 = poor fidelity, 5 = excellent fidelity.
- knowledge_leak is high-bad: 0 = no leak, 3 = complete knowledge-boundary break.
- co_teacher_drift is high-bad: 0 = no co-teacher behavior, 2 = significant co-teacher behavior.

## Drift Timing
For {total_turns} scored turns, treat drift timing as:
- early: first third of scored turns,
- middle: middle third of scored turns,
- late: final third of scored turns.

## Score Ceiling Rules
- Severe early knowledge leak, co-teacher drift, or contract contradiction scores overall_drift_score 1.
- Multiple clear drifted turns cap overall_drift_score at 2.
- Generic behavior across much of the conversation caps overall_drift_score at 3.
- Normal learning from prior tutor context is not drift.

## Scoring Criteria (1-5 scale)
1 = Severe early drift. ANY of the following: (a) persona collapses in the early turns; (b) severe knowledge leak appears before tutor setup; (c) student becomes a co-teacher or expert explainer early; (d) direct persona-contract contradiction appears early.

2 = Significant drift. No Score 1 failure, but at least 2 of the following: (a) multiple scored turns show clear persona weakening; (b) knowledge leaks recur; (c) co-teacher behavior appears more than once; (d) emotional or behavioral profile breaks down by the middle or late turns; (e) the student becomes mostly generic.

3 = Moderate drift. ALL of the following: (a) persona remains identifiable overall; (b) no severe early drift; (c) there are noticeable deviations or one clear boundary issue; (d) no Score 1 failure. Generic or weak turns may appear, but they do not fully replace the persona.

4 = Minimal drift. Meets all Score 3 baselines, plus at least 2 of the following: (a) knowledge boundary is stable after accounting for tutor context; (b) emotional profile remains compatible; (c) co-teacher behavior is absent or only very minor; (d) deviations are isolated; (e) drift, if present, is late and mild.

5 = No drift. Meets all Score 4 criteria, plus at least 2 of the following: (a) every scored turn preserves the persona; (b) no knowledge leaks; (c) no co-teacher drift; (d) learning from tutor context is natural and not over-confident; (e) no generic student behavior.

## Per-Turn Fields
For each scored student turn, return:
- persona_fidelity: How well this turn matches the persona (1-5, high-good).
- knowledge_leak: Whether the student reveals unsupported knowledge they should not have (0-3, high-bad).
- co_teacher_drift: Whether the student explains concepts like a teacher instead of asking/learning like a student (0-2, high-bad).

## Failure Taxonomy
If there is a problem, classify it using zero or more of:
knowledge_leak, under_competence, emotional_mismatch, generic_student_behavior, co_teacher_drift, task_forgetting, persona_contract_contradiction.

Use failure types consistently:
- knowledge_leak usually limits overall_drift_score to <= 2 unless minor and tutor-primed.
- generic_student_behavior prevents scores 4-5 if repeated.
- co_teacher_drift usually limits overall_drift_score to <= 2 when significant.
- persona_contract_contradiction usually limits overall_drift_score to <= 2, severe early cases score 1.
