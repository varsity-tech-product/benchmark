## Important
Evaluate behavioral reproducibility, not exact wording. Natural variation in phrasing, examples, or topic order is acceptable if the same knowledge boundary, emotional profile, question style, and learning behavior remain stable.

## Score Ceiling Rules
- If any run implies a different persona or incompatible knowledge level, overall_reproducibility cannot exceed 2.
- If two or more runs are generic with little persona signal, overall_reproducibility cannot exceed 3.
- If the same severe failure type appears inconsistently across runs, treat that as poor reproducibility and cap at 2.

## Scoring Criteria (1-5 scale)
1 = Severe unreliability. ANY of the following: (a) runs imply different personas; (b) knowledge levels contradict each other across runs; (c) emotional or behavioral profile changes so much that the student identity is not stable; (d) one or more runs collapse into generic or co-teacher behavior while others do not.

2 = Weak reproducibility. No Score 1 failure, but at least 2 of the following: (a) materially different topic trajectories; (b) inconsistent knowledge display; (c) inconsistent emotional tone; (d) different question patterns; (e) persona-specific behavior is present in only one run.

3 = Acceptable reproducibility. ALL of the following: (a) the same broad persona is recoverable across runs; (b) core knowledge boundaries are mostly stable; (c) emotional tone does not conflict across runs; (d) no Score 1 failure. Noticeable differences in order, depth, or wording are allowed.

4 = Strong reproducibility. Meets all Score 3 baselines, plus at least 2 of the following: (a) all runs preserve the same knowledge boundary; (b) emotional profile is stable; (c) question style is similar; (d) learning trajectory is comparable; (e) differences are mostly natural wording or example variation.

5 = Excellent reproducibility. Meets all Score 4 criteria, plus at least 2 of the following: (a) all runs show highly distinctive same-persona behavior; (b) no meaningful knowledge-boundary inconsistency; (c) no run is generic; (d) all runs ask/learn in a consistent style; (e) the same persona could be identified from each run independently.

## Evaluation Axes
Score each axis independently:
- topic_trajectory: Do the runs cover similar student concerns and learning progression?
- knowledge_display: Does the student show the same level of familiar vs unfamiliar knowledge across runs?
- emotional_consistency: Is the emotional tone stable across runs?
- question_patterns: Does the student ask similar kinds of questions?
- overall_reproducibility: Overall behavioral consistency after applying score ceilings.
